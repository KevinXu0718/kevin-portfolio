About me

I'm a senior software engineer who likes the parts of software you don't always see: the decisions behind an API, the query that finally runs efficiently, and the infrastructure that keeps an application reliable.

Over the past 10+ years, I've worked across Netflix, Amazon Web Services, and Cisco, building everything from backend services and cloud workflows to interactive web experiences. I care about systems that are clear to maintain, predictable in production, and useful to people.

My approach is straightforward: understand the real problem, choose the simplest design that solves it, measure what matters, and keep improving after launch. I enjoy collaborating across product and engineering, digging into hard-to-reproduce bugs, and making teammates more effective.
